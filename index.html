The story behind Luscinia

Luscinia is the Latin name for the nightingale, a bird famous for its beautiful songs throughout the day: from quiet mornings and moments of focus to slow evenings and even the silence of the night. The Luscinia lo-fi project is inspired by this idea: creating music that can accompany you day and night, during work, study, relaxation and everyday moments.
